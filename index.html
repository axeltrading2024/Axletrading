Frequently asked questions

How do I place an order?

Browse the catalog, add items to your inquiry list, then send the list to Eddy in one tap on WhatsApp or by email — the site builds the message for you. You'll get a quote with the final price, MOQ and shipping cost, and we take it from there.

What is the MOQ (minimum order quantity)?

MOQ is flexible and varies by product. Some items ship as single units for testing; better prices kick in at volume. Just ask on WhatsApp for the exact MOQ of anything you're interested in.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept bank transfer (T/T), Alipay, USDT, Western Union and Remitly — ask Eddy on WhatsApp for the option that fits your order size. Production starts after payment is confirmed.

How is shipping handled and what does it cost?

We ship worldwide. Shipping isn't a flat fee — it depends on weight, destination and speed (express air vs. sea freight). Send your inquiry list with your country and we quote the exact shipping cost before you commit to anything.
